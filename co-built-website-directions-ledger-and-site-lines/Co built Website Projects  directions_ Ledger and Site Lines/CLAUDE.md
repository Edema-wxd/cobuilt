# CoBuilt site — design template

**Master template: `CoBuilt Site Template.dc.html`** (from direction 3c). Every new page is a copy of it: keep the utility bar, header, footer, legal bar and cookie banner exactly as they are; replace only the sections between the header and the footer. Mark the current page in the nav (orange underline, #B34700 text).

## Governance (from the Corporate Digital Standards brief)
- No investment calls-to-action. Investor pathways lead to "Register your interest" and carry the licensing disclaimer. Property Investment is flagged *Informational*.
- Global nav, in this order: Home, About, Leadership, Services, Projects, Passport™, Media, Careers, Contact + "Discuss your project" button.
- Tagline: "Building Trust Through Every Brick".
- WCAG 2.2 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, hit targets ≥44px, visible form labels.
- Real figures only: 5+ delivered, 3 under development, 30+ units, 5+ locations, 10+ partners, ₦1.5B+ value created.

## Visual system
- Colours: orange #FF6600 (fills, stat band, primary buttons) — labels on orange are ink #1c1c1c, never white (2.94:1 fails); hover #E65C00; #B34700 for orange text on light backgrounds; #FF9955 for orange text on dark backgrounds; ink #1c1c1c; charcoal #333 / #141414; greys #E6E6E6, #f4f4f5, #52525b, #3f3f46.
- Type: Geist. Headlines weight 300, 40–60px, tracking −0.035em, one *italic* accent phrase (weight 400, orange). Eyebrows 11.5px uppercase, 0.28em tracking. Buttons and labels 11–12px uppercase, 0.14–0.16em tracking, weight 600.
- Shapes: square corners, 1px hairline borders, no shadows. ◈ is the value/bullet mark.
- Section padding 88px × 44px; alternate white and #f4f4f5, with dark #1c1c1c sections for emphasis.
- Reusable blocks: hero with side rail and call block, orange stat band, split about section, 3-column hairline service grid, project card (code, status chip, italic line, unit list, progress bar, location, Passport link), numbered 01–04 process row, Passport timeline, marquee, news row, register-interest form, 5-column footer.
- Geist Mono for record data only: project IDs, milestone numbers, dates, file sizes.
- Motion: none beyond 150ms colour hovers and the marquee. Loading uses static skeletons.
- Page files: CoBuilt Projects, CoBuilt Project Detail, CoBuilt Project Passport — each ends with a “Design states” board below the page.
- Styling is inline only. Image placeholders are flat #E6E6E6 blocks with a small caption. Logos: assets/cobuilt-logo-light.png and assets/cobuilt-logo-dark.png.
