Overview

A residential address built in the open.

Anthony Gardens sits on a 1.2-hectare site in Ikoyi, planned as two linked blocks around a landscaped central court. Apartments are arranged for cross-ventilation and daylight, with duplex penthouses on the upper levels.

The development is delivered under CoBuilt’s integrated model — land acquisition, feasibility, planning, development management and construction oversight — with every stage entered on the project’s public record as it happens.

Sustainability measures include rainwater harvesting, solar-assisted common areas and locally sourced masonry.
